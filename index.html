Our story

A young man, a dream, a handful of sarees.

Srikesari Silk House — once Kaiser-I-Hind Depot — was opened by Sri. M. R. Singrachar, who wanted to bring the best sarees from across the country under one roof. Bengaluru knew it soon after as the Iyengar Saree Shop.

Today it is run by the second generation, Sri. M. R. Krishna and Smt. Vani M. R. The store is still more family tradition than business: customers turned patrons, patrons turned friends.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 2–3 business days. Delivery takes 3–7 days across India.

Is the blouse piece included?

Yes, every saree comes with an unstitched blouse piece. You can also choose stitching at checkout.

What is your exchange policy?

Unworn sarees with tags can be exchanged within 7 days. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange the pickup.

How do I submit my measurements?

After ordering you'll get a link to submit blouse measurements, or our team will call you to guide you through it.

How should I care for my silk saree?

Dry clean the first time. Store folded in muslin, refold every few months along different lines, and keep away from direct sunlight.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Write to us with your address and we'll share shipping options and charges.
